How it works

(Possessive adjective) + Thing or Thing + de + Owner
Possessive adjectives — go before the thing. Agree with the thing (singular/plural). Only nuestro/vuestro change gender.

Which word do I use?

Imi / mis
you (tú)tu / tus
he / she / ustedsu / sus
wenuestro, nuestra, nuestros, nuestras
y’all (vosotros)vuestro, vuestra, vuestros, vuestras
y’all (ustedes)su / sus
theysu / sus
de + owner — “the thing of the owner.” If the owner starts with el, use del (de + el → del). Names don’t use articles.
Thing — include the article if you need it (el/la/los/las).
