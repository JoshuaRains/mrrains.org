Ser vs Estar

Video

How it works

Why? (rule) + ser or estar + Who? (form) + Predicate
Why? — choose the rule: DOCTOR → use ser. PLACE → use estar.

DOCTOR (use ser)

D — Description O — Occupation C — Characteristic T — Time/Date O — Origin R — Relationship

PLACE (use estar)

P — Position L — Location A — Action (-ing) C — Condition E — Emotion
Who? — pick the right form of the verb (who is doing the being).

Forms of ser

Isoy
you (tú)eres
he / she / ustedes
wesomos
y’all (vosotros)sois
y’all (ustedes)son
theyson

Forms of estar

Iestoy
you (tú)estás
he / she / ustedestá
weestamos
y’all (vosotros)estáis
y’all (ustedes)están
theyestán
Predicate — the rest of the idea (what, where, how, etc.).
