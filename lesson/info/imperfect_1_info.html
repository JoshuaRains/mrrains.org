Imperfect tense: first look

The imperfect describes actions in the past that were ongoing, repeated, or part of the background. A good first translation is often used to or was/were ...-ing.

Use it for habits, routines, and background details instead of one completed event.

How to build it

Take off -ar and add the imperfect ending.

  • hablar → hablaba
  • estudiar → estudiaba
  • caminar → caminábamos

Quick reminder

  • yo hablaba = I used to talk / I was talking
  • nosotros estudiábamos = we used to study / we were studying
  • The accent matters in nosotros: -ábamos
