Imperfect for habits and background

The imperfect often appears with clue words that suggest repetition or background description.

Common clue words

  • siempre = always
  • a veces = sometimes
  • todos los días = every day
  • de niño / de niña = as a child
  • generalmente = generally

Meaning

Yo estudiaba todos los días means I used to study every day. It describes a repeated past routine, not one finished event.
