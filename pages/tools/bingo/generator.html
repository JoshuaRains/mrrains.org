Printable Bingo PDF — 4 Cards per Page

Settings
Pages contain 4 cards each. Example: 24 cards → 6 pages.
A value of 5 creates a 5×5 card; 6 → 6×6, etc.
You must provide at least 25 words for a 5×5 card.
(The PDF will download automatically)
Words (paste JSON array or one word per line)
The generator uses exactly 25 words: if you paste more, only the first that many are used; if you paste fewer, you’ll see an error.
Tip for printing: Choose “US Letter, Portrait, No scaling, Narrow margins” when saving to PDF if your browser prompts for print options.
All drawing is vector-based for crisp, ink-friendly prints.
